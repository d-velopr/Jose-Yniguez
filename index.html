About Joe

Retirement Shouldn't Feel Complicated

My name is Joe Yniguez, and I'm an Executive Partner with Impact Leadership Network (ILN). For over 21 years, I've helped individuals, families, and business owners prepare for retirement through strategic financial planning focused on protection, income, and long-term stability.

I specialize in retirement income planning, fixed indexed annuities, long-term care strategies, life insurance, and wealth preservation. My passion is helping people simplify complicated financial concepts so they can make confident decisions about their future.

Today, I lead and mentor an organization of more than 160 licensed agents across multiple states. My mission is bigger than financial products — it's about creating impact, helping families build security, and developing leaders who serve their communities at a high level.

How We Build Your Retirement Plan

A clear, pressure-free process designed around your goals — and your peace of mind.

1

Free Discovery Call

We start with a complimentary conversation to understand where you are, where you want to be, and what's keeping you up at night. No pressure, no sales pitch — just an honest conversation about your retirement.

2

Custom Retirement Strategy

I analyze your complete picture — income needs, assets, healthcare costs, timeline, and legacy goals — and design a personalized plan that creates guaranteed income, protects your assets, and simplifies the complexity.
